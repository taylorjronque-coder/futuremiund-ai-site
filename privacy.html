Privacy Policy

FutureMind AI respects your privacy.

When users connect their TikTok account to FutureMind AI, the service may receive basic account information and authorization tokens needed to upload video content on the user's behalf.

FutureMind AI does not sell personal information. Data is used only to provide and operate the requested service.

Users may revoke TikTok access at any time through their TikTok account settings.

For privacy questions, contact the FutureMind AI team through the official FutureMind AI account.
